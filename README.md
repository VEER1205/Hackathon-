# Futuro AI 🔮

Your personal AI-powered guide to remove confusion and help youths choose the right career path.

## ✨ Inspiration

Navigating the complexities of career choices can be tough for young people. Many struggle with finding the right direction or identifying the skills they need to succeed. **Futuro AI** was born from the desire to solve this problem, acting as a smart advisor that offers personalized guidance to help them make informed, confident decisions.

## ⚡ Features

Futuro AI provides a robust set of tools to empower you on your career journey:

- **LinkedIn Automation** - Optimize your profile and automate networking to increase your visibility.
- **Udemy Course Recommendations** - Get personalized course suggestions to help you upskill in relevant domains.
- **Market Trends Analysis** - Stay ahead with insights into trending industries, roles, and technologies.
- **AI Chatbot** - Get instant answers to your career and skill-related questions.
- **Psychometric Test** - Assess your personality, interests, and strengths to discover suitable career paths.
- **Interview Questions Prep** - Practice for interviews with tailored questions and guidance.

## 🔄 How It Works

Futuro AI operates through a simple, guided process:

1. **User Login & Authentication:** Securely sign up or log in via **OAuth**.
2. **Psychometric Test:** Take a test that evaluates your interests, strengths, and personality traits.
3. **Career & Skill Recommendations:** Our AI suggests suitable careers, skills, and personalized Udemy courses based on your test results.
4. **Market Trends Insights:** Receive updated reports on trending jobs, industries, and market demand.
5. **LinkedIn Optimization:** Get AI-driven suggestions to improve and automate your LinkedIn presence.
6. **Chatbot Support:** Ask career-related questions and get instant, AI-powered answers.
7. **Interview Preparation:** Futuro AI generates probable interview questions tailored to your chosen career.

## 🛠 Tech Stack

Our project is built on a modern and efficient tech stack:

### Frontend:
- **HTML:** Structures the web pages.
- **CSS:** Styles the interface for a visually appealing experience.
- **JavaScript:** Adds interactivity, dynamic content, and smooth user experience.

### Backend:
- **Python:** Powers the backend logic and AI model integration.
- **FastAPI:** A high-performance web framework for building quick and reliable APIs.

### Database:
- **MongoDB:** A NoSQL database that securely stores user data, preferences, and activity, providing flexible data storage for scalability.

### Authentication:
- **OAuth:** Ensures secure login and protects user accounts.

### AI/LLM:
- **Gemini Flash 2.5:** A powerful Large Language Model (LLM) that drives the AI-powered recommendations and natural language understanding.
